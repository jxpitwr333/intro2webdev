About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started back in 2012 when our founders decided the neighborhood needed a real sourdough bakery. What started as a tiny garage setup with one oven has grown into our main shop on Main Street where neighbors hang out and get fresh bread every day.

Sourcing Philosophy

We keep things simple: local organic flour, real butter from nearby farms, and zero shortcuts.

Behind the Scenes

Watch our bakers pull fresh sourdough loaves straight out of our industrial stainless steel deck oven every morning.
