Welcome to North Star Bakery

Handmade and Community-Centered Baking

Welcome to North Star Bakery! We are a small local bakery on Main Street making fresh bread and pastries every morning. We bake everything by hand using real butter, local flour, and good ingredients. Whether you need a morning coffee and croissant before work or a couple sourdough loaves for dinner, we have got you covered.
